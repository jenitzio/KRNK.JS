# KRNK.JS 

A fast-paced, browser-based First-Person Shooter (FPS) game built purely with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Description

KRNK.JS is a thrilling multiplayer FPS experience brought to life entirely within the web browser. Leveraging the power of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it delivers a high-octane combat experience without requiring any downloads or installations. Engage in intense firefights, strategize with teammates, and climb the leaderboards in this action-packed shooter.

## Features

- **Web-Based FPS:** Experience a full-fledged first-person shooter directly in your web browser. 
- **Multiplayer Action:** Compete against other players in real-time online matches. 
- **Diverse Game Modes:** Engage in various game modes including Team Deathmatch, Battle Royale, and more. 
- **Intuitive Controls:** Master movement and combat with a familiar and responsive control scheme. 
- **Customizable Settings:** Fine-tune your gameplay experience with adjustable settings for FOV, sensitivity, graphics, and more. 
- **Character & Weapon Customization:** Personalize your appearance and loadout with unlockable skins and upgrades. 
- **Realistic Visuals:** Enjoy smooth gameplay with pixelated graphics and dynamic lighting effects. 
- **Dynamic Crosshair:** Adapt your aiming with a responsive crosshair system that indicates weapon spread and accuracy. 
- **Hit Markers & Damage Indicators:** Get immediate feedback on your shots with clear hit markers and directional damage indicators. 
- **Minimap & Killfeed:** Stay informed with a tactical minimap and a live killfeed to track the game's flow. 
- **Scoreboard:** Monitor player performance and rankings during and after matches. 
- **Offline Mode:** Practice your skills or play without an internet connection with bot matches. 

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **3D Rendering:** Three.js (via CDN)
- **Networking:** WebSockets (for real-time multiplayer)

## Installation

No installation is required to play KRNK.JS! Simply visit the game's website to start playing.

## How to Play

### Controls

- **Movement:** <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd>
- **Jump / Wall-Jump / Climb:** <kbd>Space</kbd>
- **Sprint:** <kbd>Shift</kbd>
- **Crouch / Slide:** <kbd>C</kbd>
- **Shoot:** <kbd>Left Mouse Button (LMB)</kbd>
- **Aim Down Sights (ADS):** <kbd>Right Mouse Button (RMB)</kbd>
- **Reload:** <kbd>R</kbd>
- **Switch Weapons:** <kbd>1-5</kbd> or <kbd>Mouse Scroll Wheel</kbd>
- **Throw Grenade:** <kbd>G</kbd>
- **Interact (Open Chests / Pick Up Loot):** <kbd>E</kbd>
- **Scoreboard:** <kbd>Tab</kbd>
- **Settings:** <kbd>Esc</kbd>

### Gameplay

1. **Connect:** Enter your desired player name and click "Connect to Server" or "Offline Mode".
2. **Spawn:** You will spawn into the game world.
3. **Objective:** Depending on the game mode, your objective may vary. In Team Deathmatch, eliminate opponents to score points for your team. In Battle Royale, be the last player or team standing.
4. **Loot & Upgrade:** Find weapons, ammunition, and health packs scattered around the map. Some modes allow you to spend in-game currency (earned through kills) on upgrades.
5. **Survive:** Use your movement skills, aim, and tactical awareness to outmaneuver and defeat your opponents.

## 📁 Project Structure

The project is primarily structured around a single `index.html` file, which includes all the necessary HTML, CSS, and JavaScript code. The JavaScript code is organized into logical parts (indicated by comments like `PART 2 — CONSTANTS, SETTINGS, SOUND, ENGINE, INPUT` and `PART 4 — BOTS, REMOTE PLAYERS, WEAPONS, GRENADES, HUD, CAMERA, BOOTSTRAP`) for better readability and maintainability.
